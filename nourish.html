Product Overview

👩‍🍼Target Users

Pregnant women who need practical nutrition guidance throughout pregnancy.

💡Solution

AI-powered meal recommendations, photo-based nutrient tracking, and nutrition insights that can be shared with doctors.

🛡️Problem

Pregnancy nutrition needs can be difficult to track consistently, while meal planning and understanding nutrient intake can feel overwhelming.

🎯Product Goal

Make pregnancy nutrition tracking and informed meal decisions easier.

Feature Showcase

Designed around real user needs.

03Doctor-ready Nutrition Insights

Problem

Nutrition information may be difficult to organize and communicate during healthcare consultations.

Summarized nutrition insights help users bring clearer information into conversations with healthcare professionals.

04Onboarding Experience

Problem

Users have different pregnancy needs, so generic nutrition recommendations may not be relevant to their individual circumstances.

Feature Solution

An onboarding flow collects key user information to personalize nutrition recommendations, allowing users to receive relevant guidance from their first visit.
