UI Overview

Product Overview

🫂Target Users

Students, young adults, and early-career professionals.

💬Solution

Anonymous peer support, journaling, psychologist access, and learning videos.

🧠Problem

The project highlights mental health challenges among Indonesian adolescents.

🌱Product Goal

Make mental well-being easier to maintain with accessible, judgment-free support.

Feature Showcase

Designed around real user needs.

✦ Anonymous Chattie Add screenshot: chattie-feature1.png

01Anonymous Chattie

Problem

Users may fear being judged, seen as a burden, or exposing personal thoughts when sharing emotional struggles.

Feature Solution

An anonymous chat space lets users share feelings and seek support without revealing their identity.

✦ Psychologist Chattie Add screenshot: chattie-feature2.png

02Psychologist Chattie

Problem

Professional mental health services can feel too expensive or difficult to access for students and young adults.

Feature Solution

Affordable support from licensed psychologists, including free sessions with volunteer psychologists.

✦ Chattie Journal Add screenshot: chattie-feature3.png

03Chattie Journal

Problem

Users may struggle to understand their emotional condition and lack a simple way to reflect on daily feelings.

Feature Solution

A guided daily journal supports emotional reflection with personalized AI-based insights.

✦ Chattie Learning Add screenshot: chattie-feature4.png

04Chattie Learning

Problem

Limited mental health education can leave users unsure about their condition and what steps to take next.

Feature Solution

Personalized learning videos cover emotions, coping strategies, and well-being.
